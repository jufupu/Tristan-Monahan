# 🧠 ENGINEERING DIARY

**Your Developer Productivity System — Built for VSCode, Powered by Markdown**


Welcome to your personal **Engineering Diary** — a lightweight, structured journaling system designed for coders, creators, and self-taught devs.

Track your growth. Plan your projects. Reflect on your journey. All in one clean folder — no fancy tools, just your favorite code editor.

## 🚀 How To Use This

1. **Open this folder in VSCode.**
2. Go to the `_templates/` folder.
3. Copy one of the markdown files into the right folder:

   * `daily-dev-log.md` → `01-daily-logs/`
   * `weekly-retro.md` → `02-weekly-reviews/`
   * `project-planner.md` → `03-project-journal/`
4. Rename it with today’s date or your project name.
5. Start writing. Track your habits. Reflect. Improve.

## 🧠 Why This Works

* **Lightweight:** Pure markdown, no apps to manage
* **Customizable:** Edit templates to match your workflow
* **Habit-Building:** Encourages daily and weekly reflection
* **Growth-Oriented:** See your progress over time like a developer journal


## 💡 Pro Tips

* Add `engineering-diary/` to your GitHub as a **private repo** to track your progress long-term.
* Sync with Dropbox or Google Drive for automatic backup.
* Use `⌘+B` or `Ctrl+B` in VSCode to toggle the file tree quickly.
